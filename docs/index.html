Anime Release Calendar

A browser extension that brings a weekly anime release calendar back, one click away inside Crunchyroll's own menu.

Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Crunchyroll, AniList or AniDachi. All trademarks belong to their respective owners.

What it does

  • Weekly calendar — seven columns, the current day highlighted, the real date on every header.
  • Browse other weeks — step back to catch up, or forward to see what is coming.
  • Direct episode links — opens that exact episode, not a search page.
  • Queue marking — a discreet check mark on series already in your Crunchyroll queue.
  • Filtering — everything, only your queue, or a single Crunchylist.
  • English and Portuguese (Brazil), switchable in the page.

Privacy

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no account, no remote code. Nothing is sent to the developer. Your Crunchyroll credential is never requested from you, never read from cookies, kept in memory only, and sent only back to crunchyroll.com.
